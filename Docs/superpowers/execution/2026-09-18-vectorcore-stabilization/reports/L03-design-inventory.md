## Curve fit and coordinate scale

UMAP.swift:314–384 fits f(x)=1/(1+a*x^(2b)) on 300 evenly spaced x in [0,3*spread], target1 below minDist and exp(-(x-minDist)/spread) above. It starts a=b=1, takes at most200 damped normal-equation updates, and returns Float(a),Float(b) after any determinant, damping, small-step or iteration exit. No success flag, objective/stationarity test, coefficient representability check or failure error is present. An unchanged initial guess can be returned as if fitted. The derivatives in the code are the derivatives of this stated objective; unit-dependent conditioning and unchecked termination are the main issue, not a different target equation.

With t=x/spread and r=minDist/spread, the target is1 for t<r and exp(-(t-r)) otherwise on [0,3]. Fit A=a*spread^(2b) and b in dimensionless coordinates. Recovering a requires log(a)=log(A)-2*b*log(spread). Root must choose between explicit rejection when the coefficient/evaluation representation cannot support the requested scale and carrying a normalized/log representation into optimizer evaluation. Finiteness alone does not establish positive nonzero a after Float conversion. Choose a bounded convergence criterion that distinguishes actual convergence from singular/damping/exhaustion failure; SSE need not be zero because the target is not exactly this two-parameter family. A small accepted step alone can also represent stagnation. Preserve the fixed grid/objective unless explicitly changing the contract.

This correction does not imply scale-equivariant layouts. Initialization uses [-10,10], PCA rescales to max-absolute10 plus1e-4 jitter, gradient clipping is +/-4, and repulsion contains0.001; learningRate and stochastic scheduling remain additional scales. Fit-scale tests should compare b, A or normalized kernel samples, and independently recomputed SSE, not complete layouts across units.

## Smooth-kNN equation, solvability and output representation

FuzzySimplicialSet.swift:46–91 computes global mean over all directed entries; per row rho is the smallest strictly positive distance (or0), and target T=log2(k+1). The search solves S(sigma)=sum exp(-max(0,d-rho)/sigma)=T, starts sigma1 in absolute units and expands/bisects for64iterations, then applies floor0.001 times row mean when rho>0, else global mean. Search exhaustion is not checked. rho and sigma narrow to Float before build recomputes memberships.

Let z count entries with d<=rho. For a row with positive shifted distances, S is continuous and increasing from z at sigma->0+ to k at sigma->infinity. A unique positive finite solution exists when z<T<k. T<=z is unattainable at positive finite sigma (or a limiting endpoint), which includes many tied/duplicate rows. For k=1, T=k=1 and the only membership is always1; sigma is not identifiable. All-zero rows have S=k for all positive sigma. Isolated rows currently retain rho=sigma0 and no edges. Duplicate neighbor IDs are legal; calibration counts all stored entries, while later fusion takes max per directed pair. Decide whether to preserve that distinction explicitly.

Root must specify tied/unidentifiable/isolated behavior, floor behavior, and residual success before versus after a dominating floor. A floor can intentionally move even a solvable row away from its defining target, so universal post-floor residual assertions would be contradictory. Scale the bracket or normalized shifted distances; a bounded search still needs a residual/failure policy. If retaining Float sigma, both overflow and underflow matter. `build`:113 currently assigns weight1 whenever sigma==0, even for strictly positive shifted distance; underflow of a positive sigma can therefore corrupt topology. Either preserve a widened/normalized bandwidth internally or define supported-range rejection/limiting membership semantics. Float output spacing must be separated from the search convergence tolerance.

A primary closed-form fixture is distances[s,2s]: rho=s, T=log2(3), w=T-1, sigma=-s/log(w). The floor is inactive. Use exact power-of-two scales with representable values, unequal degrees, isolated rows, tied nearest distances, pure duplicates and duplicate IDs. Independently calculate directed weights and union w1+w2-w1*w2, checking expected dropped underflow edges separately from equation convergence. Current canonical packing uses Int32-bounded point indices, and sorts final directed pairs; retain order/symmetry/max-duplicate rules unless explicitly changed.

## Boundary inventory

- UMAPConfig.validate:98 checks neighbors>=2, epochs>=1, negativeSampleRate>=0; spread>0, 0<=minDist<=spread, learningRate>0. NaN generally fails comparisons, but +Inf learningRate and spread pass, and minDist=spread=+Inf passes. All three Float fields need explicit finite tests through both public overloads. neighbors is documented unused with supplied graphs but still globally validated; decide whether to preserve that existing policy. initialization is an exhaustive enum and seed is UInt64, with no invalid numeric state.
- Both UMAP overloads only require dimensions>=1 before `n*dimensions` at188/215/225 and flat allocation; PCA init has n*m at276. Check product and Float bytes before allocation/indexing. n>=2 is checked only in shared tail after initialization; move/define ordering consistently to avoid work before inevitable rejection. Very large dimensions must not reach PCA/GEMM or random allocation before representability checks. No arbitrary workload cap is needed to repair arithmetic.
- Provided coordinates:207–221 checks row count and individual widths, then copies values unchanged; no finite/range checks. Validate complete shape before coordinate values under a chosen typed policy. Root must choose rejection versus normalization for extreme finite coordinates. Output finite checks alone cannot detect an intermediate NaN that Float min/max clipping masks into finite updates; retaining current Float gradients needs a declared safe-domain or intermediate-error policy rather than a universal robust-computation claim.
- PCA init:285 forms Float10/maxAbs; tiny nonzero finite projections can make this scale infinite even though L02 returns finite coordinates. Compute the bounded ratio before multiplication in adequate precision or reject; also validate jitter/result. This is a reachable derived-initialization path, distinct from caller-provided coordinates.
- Attract/repulse:475–516 subtract and square in Float; finite1e30-scale differences overflow squared distance, and coefficient products/powers can produce Inf/NaN. Huge finite learningRate can overflow clipped updates. Coincident attraction is a no-op; coincident repulsion adds4*alpha each coordinate. Preserve or explicitly change these policies; do not silently normalize supplied coordinates without documenting their changed meaning relative to minDist/spread.
- optimize:410 prunes using Float(maxWeight/epochs), cadence:420 divides maxWeight/w in Float before widening, negative cadence divides by Double(rate), and due:450 converts a floating quotient to Int unchecked. Double(Int.max) rounds to2^63 on this platform; comparing <=Double(Int.max) does not protect Int conversion. A pure conversion helper should reject nonfinite/out-of-range values before conversion (strict upper bound or checked exact conversion after chosen rounding), preserving truncation/schedule meaning. Decide how to handle mathematically excessive due counts; silent saturation would alter sampling, and simply accepting a giant representable loop is not bounded work. Huge epochs/rate are caller-chosen work unless root explicitly adds a documented bound; test arithmetic helpers instead of running them. Float epoch annealing loses resolution for huge counts; no practical runtime promise follows from arithmetic checks.
- KNNGraph.init:42 checks Int32 point bounds, n+1 offset count, endpoints, and parallel entry lengths. It checks each row's monotonicity immediately before iterating that row, so a later decrease can be reached only after an earlier row dereferences outside entry arrays. First validate every offset in0...nnz and monotonicity, then any edge access. Preserve empty/uneven/unsorted rows, duplicate IDs, no self-loop, finite nonnegative distances. n+1 is safe after the existing Int32 bound.
- Fuzzy build reserves2*canonical.count:124 without checked multiplication; output can contain up to2*nnz directed entries. Include count/element-byte representability for touched scratch/output buffers, independently of allocation success or L04's future memory envelope. k+1 in smooth target:65 is another addition to review even though actual Float distance storage already limits realizable row counts; Double(k)+1 avoids the Int addition.
- Public bruteForce has n*k outputs:142/143, blockRows*d:152, blockRows*n GEMM output:159, and row offsets i*k:193. Its pointCount Int32 bound is enforced only by the final graph constructor, after Int32(j):176 and LP64 GEMM. L02 protects shared flatten product/row shape but deliberately does not supply all KNN-specific GEMM/count validation. Root should explicitly authorize local preflight guards if including this same arithmetic class, without changing scores. Input NaN/Inf and extreme finite Gram arithmetic have no dedicated early boundary; choose documentation versus finite-input strengthening separately, rather than accidentally inheriting PCA finite policy.

## Gram conditioning and minimal root decisions

KNNGraph.bruteForce:134–180 accumulates Float norms and GEMM dots, computes norm_i+norm_j-2*dot, and clamps negative scores to0 before selection. It exhaustively scans these computed scores; it is not a reliable direct-distance oracle under offsets. Canonical [10000,10002,10001] demonstrates the distinction. Preserve the documented conditioning limitation unless root deliberately opens the P02-dependent algorithm work. Reranking only the already-selected k candidates cannot recover a true nearest candidate discarded by cancellation. An independent Double direct-difference fixture can characterize the known divergence without making the old incorrect score a correctness oracle or requiring a repair in L03.

Before dispatch, root decisions needed are: (1) dimensionless curve coefficient/evaluation representation and convergence/failure; (2) smooth bandwidth representation, solvability/floor/underflow policy; (3) finite/extreme init and update policy including PCA rescaling; (4) explicit checked CSR contract and synchronized docs; (5) integer due-count/count-byte rejection semantics and which KNN preflights are authorized; (6) preserve Gram algorithm with a qualified exactness label. These are bounded mathematical/API decisions, not performance targets.

Independent acceptance should combine analytic curve/kernel and smooth equations, generated tiny malformed CSR offsets with valid final endpoints, NaN/Inf tables for each Float field and each initialization route, pure integer/byte/conversion boundary tables, and small ordinary deterministic controls. Use bounded children only for actual trap-risk baseline reproductions, never giant allocations/loops. New strengthened boundaries remain separate from historical qualified NUM-25/26/28 exposure. Source-backed observations here do not reproduce the historical crashes or establish a downstream runtime claim.
