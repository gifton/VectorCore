## What the failure means

The benchmark generates signed finite LCG-derived rows using 24 input bits and per-row seeds, calls real MatrixDistance, checks every pair for N<=8, and forms references by Double coordinate differences. Correctness.vector reports finite error statistics with denominator max(abs(reference),1e-12). Its `.valid` status means valid finite observations, not that strict tolerance is met. The new test's maxRelError<=1e-6 assertion adds a kernel-accuracy guarantee absent from the brief.

MatrixDistance separately computes Float row norms with vDSP and Float cross terms with SGEMM, then evaluates cross+norm+norm and clamps negatives to zero. Distinct reduction paths can disagree even for identical rows. A positive cancellation residual remains positive after that clamp. Coordinate-difference self-reference is exactly zero for these finite identical Float inputs; its relative statistic therefore divides residual by1e-12. The reported 30,517,578.125 and61,035,156.25 would correspond respectively to absolute residuals2^-15 and2^-14 if their maxima occur at zero-reference pairs. That is a mathematical interpretation, not an independently observed diagonal attribution: worker's retained entry-level output/reference records must establish the actual offending pairs. A width768 pass on this build establishes neither general diagonal exactness nor a cross-platform pass promise.
