# Code of conduct

VectorCore welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Participation in this project's issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other project spaces is subject to these expectations.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience. Critique ideas and code with specific
  evidence, and make room for questions and different technical perspectives.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries. Ask before sharing another person's
  private messages or identifying information.
- Accept constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and help resolve conflict.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and respect maintainer decisions
  about scope and support capacity.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual attention or imagery,
personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.
Disagreement over technical choices is welcome; targeting a person is not.

## Reporting concerns

Contact the maintainer privately using the email address listed on
[@gifton's GitHub profile](https://github.com/gifton). Include the relevant links,
a description of what happened, and any immediate concerns. Do not post private
or sensitive details in a public issue. The profile is the source for the
current contact address.

The maintainer will review reports and limit disclosure to what is necessary
to investigate and respond. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
Handling is best-effort, without a guaranteed response deadline or a formal
independent appeals process. You may also report violations of GitHub's own
rules using GitHub's reporting tools, including concerns involving a maintainer.

Report suspected software vulnerabilities through the separate
[security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may clarify expectations, request a correction, remove content,
close a discussion, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation,
depending on the conduct and its impact. Serious threats or harassment may
result in immediate restriction. Where practical, the maintainer will explain
the action privately and consider relevant additional information.

These expectations also apply when someone represents this project in an
official capacity elsewhere. Fork maintainers must provide their own reporting
contact and enforcement policy before adopting this document.
